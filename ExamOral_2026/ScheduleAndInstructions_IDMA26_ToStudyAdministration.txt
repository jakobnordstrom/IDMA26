EXAM INSTRUCTIONS

The oral re-exam will be held in the Algorithms and Complexity Section premises at Vibenshuset at Lyngbyvej 2, right at the metro station Vibenshus Runddel.

As you exit the metro station, Vibenshuset is the building just across the street to your right on the corner. The offices of the Algorithms and Complexity Section are on the 4th floor (four flights of stairs above the ground floor).

After you have entered Vibenshuset, go straight through the building (past the reception area) until the end (at a coffee shop), where you turn right and walk down the hallway until you reach the lift to your right. Take the lift (or the stairs just behind it) to the 4th floor. As you exit the lift, turn right and walk to the door, which should have signs for BARC and QMATH. This door requires an access card, but you can ring the BARC doorbell to get someone to open.

When you have entered, walk straight through the first open space and continue through the kitchen area, and then you have reached the premises of the Algorithm and Complexity Section. There are lots of tables close to a second kitchen area, and if you wait there we will come and pick you up for the exam. 

Please make sure to arrive for your exam 5-10 minutes in advance, so that we can start on time. Also please note that although it can be hard to stick exactly to the schedule during a long exam day like this, you have to show up in time for your exam --- late arrival counts as a no-show.
